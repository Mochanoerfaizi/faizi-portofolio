Hi, I’m Mochamad Noer Faizi Supriatna Putra

Quality Assurance Engineer · Junior Developer · Administrative Support

I am a detail-oriented QA Engineer with a strong passion for ensuring software quality and delivering great user experiences. I have solid experience in Manual Testing, UI Testing, API Testing, and Automation Testing.

I believe quality is not just about finding bugs, but about building confidence in every release. I enjoy working in collaborative teams, creating effective test strategies, and continuously learning new technologies.

Starting from QA, I am used to looking at a product from the user’s perspective while also understanding how it is built. I have built web applications with Laravel and Vue.js, an Android application with Kotlin, and an AI-based tool with Python.

Beyond IT, I am comfortable using Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) to create documents, process data, prepare reports, and deliver presentations. My experience as a Clinic Administrator trained me in data recording, customer service, and administrative discipline. This combination makes me ready to work across different fields, not only in the technology industry.

I am always eager to take on new challenges and contribute to building user-focused digital products.
